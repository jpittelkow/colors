# ADR audit — 2026-11-22

Audit of ADRs 0001–0026 (all written the same day, before any code). Two claims were checked on the web; the rest is
a read for contradictions, wrong assumptions and gaps. Nothing in the ADRs has been changed yet.

## Wrong or unsupported claims

1. **0016 — there is no YouTube Kids TV app to copy.** Google removed the standalone YouTube Kids app from TVs in July
   2024; on a TV it is now a kids profile inside the main YouTube app. The ADR's screen list (category bar, visible
   timer, parent gate, time-up screen) describes the phone/tablet app. The reference must be chosen: the kids profile
   in the YouTube TV app, or the tablet app adapted to a remote. "Take screenshots on one of our TVs" cannot be done as
   written.
2. **0018 layer 2 — lock task mode is not available to a sideloaded app.** It needs the app to be device owner, which
   on a TV with a Google account signed in means a factory reset. The ADR lists it as an option and rejects device
   owner as an alternative in the same file. Screen pinning has no TV interface.
3. **0018 layer 1 — "set as home screen" is not an app setting on Google TV.** There is no launcher picker; it takes
   adb (`set-home-activity` or disabling the stock launcher) or an accessibility-service trick like Projectivy's. It
   belongs with the adb runbook, and on one Chromecast Projectivy keeps Home by the owner's choice.
4. **0008 — "direct play" from Plex is the exception, not the rule.** Local files are forced to H.264 1080p (0005),
   but Plex movies are often HEVC, 4K or DTS, which the TVs may not play. Proxying a Plex transcode (HLS session,
   keep-alives) is much harder than proxying a file. The ADR treats it as a footnote.

## Contradictions between ADRs

5. **0011 vs 0012 — is time watched-time or wall-clock?** 0011 counts watched time from heartbeats (pause is free).
   0012 says pausing "still uses up the wall-clock limits". Neither says which limits are which. Needed: daily
   allowance and session = watched time; one-off limit and schedule = wall clock.
6. **0010 vs 0011 — switching profile defeats the allowance.** Profile switching is free and the guest profile has its
   own allowance, so a child out of time picks his brother or Guest. 0010 calls this acceptable; it undoes the main
   promise of 0011. Needs a per-TV daily cap, or guest behind the parent gate, or both.
7. **0026 vs itself — lock-down timing.** G7 (block real YouTube) is listed in M6, and the text says it happens after
   M2.
8. **0026 vs 0013/0016/0022 — tags are needed before AI exists.** The category bar (M4) and tag rules (M7) need tags;
   AI tagging is M8 and no milestone gives the admin page a way to tag by hand.
9. **0019 vs 0022 — does "put this on for them" obey the rules?** A parent pushing a video from the phone either goes
   through the pipeline (and may be refused) or bypasses it. Not stated. Same for the grown-up profile.
10. **0006 — short-lived stream token vs long videos.** The player makes range requests for the whole length; a token
    that expires mid-movie breaks playback. Needs a lifetime tied to the item, or renewal.

## Gaps inside ADRs

11. **0004 — cookies and rate limits.** A cookies file from a real Google account risks that account being flagged;
    it should say "throwaway account only". Bulk channel imports also hit YouTube's bot checks; the ADR has no pacing
    rule beyond one-at-a-time.
12. **0005 — "delete watched" with two children.** Watched by whom? One boy finishing a video should not delete it for
    the other.
13. **0005/0023 — iGPU risk understated.** A third VAAPI user beside the other services raises the chance of the iGPU
    wedge that hung the home server on 2026-11-19. Conversions should be CPU by default or idle-hours only.
14. **0011 — same profile on two TVs at once** is undefined (count double, or refuse the second).
15. **0012/0022 — dead ends.** Learn-first plus time-fit can leave nothing playable (no learning video fits). Together
    mode does not say whose optional rules apply. The looping channel (0013) has no length, so it vanishes under any
    limit.
16. **0014 — the untagged rule is ambiguous.** "Treats an untagged item as not matching" can be read either way; it
    should say untagged items are hidden while a restricting tag rule is active.
17. **0015 — the APK signing key** is not mentioned. Losing it means uninstalling and re-pairing every TV.
18. **0025 vs 0002 — share target only works at home.** The server is not reachable from outside, so sharing from the
    phone away from home fails unless a queue or VPN is added. HTTPS "decided at deploy" also affects the TV app
    (cleartext vs certificate trust), so it deserves its own ADR.
19. **0013/0016 — the boys cannot read well.** Nothing says how a non-reader searches or understands a "locked" reason
    (voice, icons, spoken prompts).

## Missing ADRs

20. **Who are we defending against?** No statement of the boys' ages and what they can do (press Home, switch input,
    use another app, another device). 0018's layers cannot be judged without it.
21. **Database migrations and backup/restore** (0003 and 0023 only mention backup).
22. **Testing**, including a guard test that every TV endpoint goes through the pipeline (WhatsOn ADR 0021 style).
23. **Thumbnails, sprites and artwork caching** (referenced in 0015, owned by no ADR).
24. Already listed as undecided in the index: Shorts feed, break prompts, family-channel upload, tablets, travel.

## Bookkeeping

25. **Build order leaves 12 ideas unplaced:** A4, A9, B9, B12, C2, C8, C12, F8, F12, F13, F14, J8. The index covers
    all 120.
26. **Several ADRs hold many decisions** (0004, 0012, 0013, 0018). Superseding one part later means rewriting the
    whole file. 0012 and 0018 are worth splitting.
27. **Five calls are mine, not the owner's:** new codebase (0003), own streaming (0006), admin password (0018), own name
    and artwork (0016), HA via REST (0020).

## What holds up

Server-side clock (0011), thin client (0017), single pipeline (0022), approval model (0009), skip-at-playback (0024),
local-only history (0021), control socket (0019) and the deployment shape (0023) are consistent with each other and
with the idea list.

The owner: copy a kids profile in the YouTube TV app; do not worry about profile hopping; the boys are 2 and 4.
