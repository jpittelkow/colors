# Navigation audit of the parents' page, 2026-11-23 (second pass)

The first audit (`ui-audit-2026-11-23.md`) gave the page its four tabs (ADR 0037). This pass looks only at
**where things live and how you get to them**, after a day of use. Walked on the local dev server at phone width
(375 px) and desktop width (1280 px).

## How you reach each screen today

| Tab | Screens behind it |
|---|---|
| Library | Search, Add (Browse Plex, Import, Ideas), Subscribe, a channel, a video |
| Approve | (one screen, two panes on a computer) |
| Kids | Each boy's page → Limits and rules, This week, Picture and name. **The household:** Vacation mode, TVs, Profiles |
| Settings | **Server:** Downloads, Jobs, Library size. **Safety:** Blocked words, Skipping inside videos. **Connections:** Plex, AI, Home Assistant. **This page:** Appearance, Change password, Sign out |

Plus the activity pill on every screen, whose panel links to Downloads and Jobs.

## Findings

The owner's three, built in this pass (ADR 0037, revision 2026-11-23):

- **N1. Downloads and Jobs are two taps deep.** They are the pages you check most when something is going on
  (is it downloading, did the channel check run), yet they sit under Settings › Server next to the library size
  limit. The activity pill is the only shortcut. **Change:** each gets its own place in the nav, after Kids. Like
  Approve, each carries a count when something needs a look: failed downloads on Downloads, failed jobs on Jobs.
  Their top-bar links to each other go, since both are now one tap away.
- **N2. The household sits under the boys' daily controls.** Kids is the screen for right now (who is watching,
  +10 min, end now); Vacation mode, TVs and Profiles are setup you change rarely. **Change:** they move to a
  Household group at the top of Settings. The "Add the boys" button on an empty Kids screen still goes to
  Profiles. A boy's own "Picture and name" stays on his page.
- **N3. The Library search box and Add are on separate lines.** On a computer the top bar held only the Add
  button, with the search box on its own row under it. **Change:** one row, search box then Add, in the bar that
  stays at the top while you scroll. On a phone the wordmark keeps its own line above that row.

Found on the way. The owner picked N4 and N5, built the same evening; N6-N8 are notes, no change.

- **N4. Two Settings rows open the same page.** "Blocked words" and "Skipping inside videos" both go to the Safety
  screen. Either one row ("Safety: blocked words, parts to skip") or two real screens. **Built:** one row, "Blocked words and parts to skip", showing how many of each are set.
- **N5. Library size is alone in Settings › Server now.** It is about room for downloads and the Downloads page
  already shows "storage full". It could move onto the Downloads page, and Server would go away. **Built:** the size bar, free space and the limit with its own Save are the last section of Downloads; the "library is full" banner jumps to it; `#storage` still opens it.
- **N6. Six places in the phone's bottom bar.** At 375 px each is 62 px wide; the labels fit at 12 px, but six is
  the most the bar should take. If a seventh ever comes, Downloads and Jobs should share one "Activity" place
  with a Downloads | Jobs switch, not shrink further.
- **N7. The activity pill and the two new nav places overlap.** The pill is still worth keeping as the live
  glance on every screen; its "Downloads → / Jobs →" footer links are now optional.
- **N8. Plex is in two places on purpose** (connect under Settings › Connections, browse under Library › Add, per
  ADR 0037). No change; noted so it is not "fixed" by accident.

## Back button

The back arrow uses the browser's history, and falls back to a fixed parent when a screen is opened directly
(for example from a TV's QR code, ADR 0039). With TVs and Profiles moving, their fallback parent is now
Settings, and Downloads and Jobs, being nav places, have no back arrow.
