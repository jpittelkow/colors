# Parents' page: UI audit, 2026-11-23

The owner asked for a full audit after using the live page: adding from Plex is in a weird spot, the Add page seems
empty and could be combined with the library, and it is not clear what the inbox does. This is a walk through
every screen as a parent on a phone, with what is wrong and what to do about it.

**Built the same day** as ADR 0037 (four tabs, one search, the desktop layout). The tables below are the findings
as they stood before that.

## The short version

The screens grew one feature at a time, so the page is organised by **how the server works** (add, inbox,
downloads, jobs) instead of by **what a parent wants to do**: see what the boys can watch, add more, say yes or no
to new things, and manage their time. Most of the problems below come from that.

## Proposed shape

Four tabs, named for what a parent does, with adding as one obvious button.

1. **Library** (home). Everything the boys can watch, and the only search box. Typing searches three places at
   once and shows them as sections: **In your library**, **On YouTube**, **In Plex**. A clear **Add** button opens
   the other ways in: paste a link, browse Plex, import from YouTube Kids, ideas from the AI. Filters: All / Sam
   / Alex, and Channels / Videos / Plex. Each channel shows a count and a small mark for subscribed.
   This removes the Add tab (A1, A2, A3, A5).
2. **Approve** (was Inbox). One line at the top says what it is: "New videos wait here until you say yes."
   Grouped by channel, with **Approve** and **Not for them** as words, plus "Approve all from this channel" and
   "Always approve this channel". Downloads still in progress or failed move out to Downloads. Ideas from the AI
   live under Add, not here (A4, C1).
3. **Kids**. Each boy's card opens to everything about him: watching now, time left, limits, this week, his
   picture and name. Vacation mode and the TVs move here (A7, A8, B6).
4. **Settings** (was More), in four groups: **Server** (Downloads, Jobs, storage size), **Safety** (blocked words,
   skipping), **Connections** (Plex, AI, Home Assistant), **This page** (appearance, password, sign out) (A6, A8).

Smaller changes that go with it:

- **+** on a search result asks "For Sam, Alex or both?" once and remembers the answer (B1).
- **Subscribe** becomes a short summary with the button at the top and the options folded away (B2).
- **Plex** splits: connecting stays in Settings; browsing and allowing moves under Add (B3, and the owner's "weird
  spot").
- Video and channel screens get titles, plain words ("Hide from the boys", "Parts to skip"), and the main actions
  at the top (B4, B5, B7).
- One chip style where selected is unmistakable, labelled row buttons, real empty states, room at the bottom of
  every screen so the pill never covers a button (C1 to C6).

## A desktop layout too

The owner, same day: the page is mobile first, but a proper desktop version would be good to have. Today a computer
just shows the phone column, 640 px wide, in the middle of an empty screen (finding D1). Same page, same code,
laid out for the width it has:

- **From about 900 px wide:** the four tabs become a **sidebar on the left** with the wordmark on top, and the
  activity pill moves to the bottom of that sidebar. The content area uses the full width.
- **Library:** channels and videos as a real grid, 4 to 6 across, with the filters in a row above it.
- **Approve:** two panes. The list on the left, the selected video on the right with a preview player, who it is
  for, and big Approve / Not for them buttons. Arrow keys move through the list; A approves.
- **Search results:** a grid, with the library, YouTube and Plex sections side by side when there is room.
- **Downloads:** the history becomes a table (title, channel, size, time taken, when, status).
- **Kids:** the boys' cards side by side.
- **Settings:** the groups in two columns.
- Forms (subscribe, limits, a profile) stay a narrow, centred column: they read better that way at any size.

Phones keep the bottom bar and the single column exactly as proposed above.

## What stays

The Kids cards, the Downloads page and pill (just rebuilt), the profile editor, the design system's colours and
type. This is a re-arrangement and a wording pass, not a new look.

## Size

About a day and a half of work on `server/app/static/admin/app.js` and its CSS (the desktop layout is roughly
the extra half), and small API additions (one search that also covers Plex
and the library; a library list with filters). No database changes. It should go in as ADR 0037 (navigation by
what a parent does) so the reasons are recorded.
